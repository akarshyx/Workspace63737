---
name: Keno payout policy
description: Fairness and bankroll rules for the casino's Keno payout schedule.
---

Keno outcomes must remain cryptographically random and independent of bet size or player identity. House profit is an expected long-run margin created by the published RTP and a hard maximum payout, not by forcing a particular player's high bet to lose.

**Why:** A guaranteed loss for a high bet would make the game outcome-driven rather than a fair, auditable Keno draw and would undermine player trust.

**How to apply:** When changing Keno probabilities or multipliers, calculate the exact hit distribution first, keep the draw independent, cap the top payout, and verify expected RTP stays at or below the configured target after rounding.
